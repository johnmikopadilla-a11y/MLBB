About Mobile Legends

Game basics

Victory & defeat

Two teams of five fight across three lanes and a jungle. A match is won by destroying the enemy base, so every kill, objective and rotation should push toward that goal.

Lanes & jungle

Gold lane, mid lane and EXP lane each have a tower line to defend. The jungler farms monsters, takes the Turtle and Lord, and ganks lanes that need help.

Gold, levels & items

Farm minions and monsters to earn gold, buy items in the shop, and level up skills as you gain experience. Build to counter what the enemy team is doing.

Hero roles

  • Tank
  • Fighter
  • Assassin
  • Mage
  • Marksman
  • Support

Everyday mechanics

  • Hero skills

    Every hero has a passive and several active skills. Learn the combo before you play ranked.

  • Equipment & builds

    Pick items that fit your role and the enemy lineup, then adjust as the match changes.

  • Battle spells

    Choose one spell before the match. Flicker, Retribution and Sprint each suit different heroes.

  • Emblems

    Emblem talents give small permanent bonuses. Match the set to your hero's role.
